# Customer lifecycle and entitlement operations

Migration 20261101054139 follows product registry and prior identity/security migrations. /admin/customers adds persisted organization state, impact preview and atomic product grant/subscription-term editing. Existing organization status, subscription and grant endpoints delegate to the same transactional function instead of separately writing an audit afterward. All operations are platform-admin-only with fresh database actor checks; organization admins/product roles confer no platform access.

Every mutation requires the displayed organization version and explicit confirmation. A stale form conflicts; no silent last-writer overwrite occurs. Preview reports active memberships, current product sessions and outstanding invitations without exposing credentials. Suspension/closure revokes customer product sessions, Music codes and pending invitations/outbox jobs in the transaction. Closure is terminal in this workflow. Reactivation does not recreate sessions, invitation capabilities or memberships. Existing effective entitlement/role/organization checks remain mandatory on requests.

A database BEFORE INSERT guard locks the active organization while creating a product session. Lifecycle changes lock the organization before revocation, preventing a concurrent login from inserting a session after suspension. Native acceptance exercises suspension with a concurrent product-session insert; the insert fails closed. Portal sessions remain separate; suspending one customer does not globally disable a multi-customer account.

A combined entitlement transaction saves one product's grant and subscription terms, revokes its old sessions/codes and commits an audit together. Subscription status is active/trial/past_due/cancelled/expired; grants are enabled/suspended/revoked. Trial requires a future end, dates must be ordered, and plan codes are bounded. Existing starts_at is retained when omitted. Future starts/expired ends continue to deny access. This is entitlement administration, not billing/payment/invoice processing. Individual membership and product role are still separate requirements; enabling a grant never assigns roles.

Customer directories use UUID keyset pages of 100 and direct ID reads. Product registry uses code keyset pages and direct ID reads. Detail subcollections remain bounded as documented in CUSTOMER_GOVERNANCE.md; broad search/export, retention and complete history pagination remain M9 work. Customer entitlement product selection is bounded to the returned registry page; use approved API IDs for products beyond that selection until broader selection search is implemented.

Failure/recovery: audit failure rolls the mutation back, stale versions require reload, invalid transition/terms never partially commit grant/subscription changes. Keep migration history/audit/outbox data. Forward repair and verified backup restore replace destructive down migrations. Do not deploy a backend requiring this function/trigger until it is installed and verified in the approved target. No hosted installation, real Auth/email/deployment or production session mutation was performed in this implementation.
