## Normal localhost workflow

Pull main, run `npm ci`, restart with `npm run dev` and refresh. Sign in at `http://localhost:3000/login` with the existing owner/HR account. In **People → Employees**, open the employee's account-link action and choose **Create login invitation**. New onboarding completion also has **Set up employee login**. Copy the once-shown private link and deliver only to the named employee. No SMTP or email delivery is automatic. The employee chooses a password, accepts and signs in at the normal portal. Existing accounts continue through consent linking, not password reset.

Leave employee status as Onboarding until an authorized manager deliberately activates the roster. Activation, assigned work/projects and owner-only HR appointment remain separate. Verify own profile/employment persists after reload; HR governance, the staff directory, other employees and unassigned customer products must remain inaccessible to an ordinary employee. Check expired/cancelled/reissued links and log out before switching test accounts. No fixture credentials or separate demo server are needed for this actual workflow.
